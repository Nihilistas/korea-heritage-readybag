Site tickets (adult tourist)

Foreigner ages 19–64, one person. Official 궁능 / city / temple tariffs checked 16 Sep 2026. Almost nowhere sells a dearer “with guide” ticket. Jongmyo on a weekday is the exception: the guide is how you are allowed in, at the same ₩1,000.

Jongmyo weekday is a time slot, not a price upgrade

Mon/Wed/Thu/Fri you enter with a 해설사 at a posted clock (English 10:00, 12:00, 14:00, 16:00; about 1 h). Sunday 15 Nov is walk-in. Palaces are the opposite: buy ₩3,000 and wander, or join a free 정규해설 if you want. Changdeokgung 후원 (₩5,000, timed) is not this trip.

English 해설 vs the clocked days

Posted English exists as a timetable only at Jongmyo, Changdeokgung grounds, and Gyeongbokgung. Everywhere else is Korean 정기해설, a phone reservation, or a pamphlet. Chasing a guide at every pad does not break the whole trip. It breaks Ganghwa, Pocheon, and Yeoncheon (rare buses), and it can miss Gongju’s 770 / KTX.

Palace Sunday works if you reverse the order

Current plan is walk-in Jongmyo at 09:17, then Changdeokgung, then Gyeongbokgung. For English: Changdeokgung 10:15 (about 50 min) → Jongmyo 12:00 (about 1 h) → Gyeongbokgung 13:30 (60–90 min). Keep today’s order and you wait for Changdeokgung 13:15 and Gyeongbokgung 15:30 (November last entry 16:00). 후원 English (10:30 / 11:30 / 14:30 / 15:30 in November, about 70 min, extra ₩5,000) still does not fit.

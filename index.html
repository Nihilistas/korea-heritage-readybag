21 stamps this trip (ready bag at 20)

Arrive ICN Friday 6 Nov 2026 evening. Sleep in Jongno. Saturday is AREX to T1 for the book and three 홍보관 stamps, then continue to Gimpo Jangneung. Sunday is Jeondeungsa and the Ganghwa dolmen. The bag needs 20 stamps; this outing is 21 so Gongsanseong is collected with Magoksa toward the 76.

Magoksa is two stamps, airport is three

Magoksa: press the same pad onto 백제고도의 길 and 산사의 길. Airport: finish the three T1 홍보관 missions, then stamp at the H-zone table. Later dual sites (Donam, Dosan, Bongjeongsa) are not on this trip.

What counts

Tourist souvenirs use stamp count only. Plus pads do not count. Official 셀프 체험존 only — palace souvenir stamps do not. Gongsanseong is the 21st stamp so you do not return to Gongju for the 76. Buyeo extras are optional this trip if you still have daylight.
